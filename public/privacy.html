Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Summary: We collect only what we need, store it securely, and never sell your data. You own your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Ask Juan form, we collect your name, email address, and message content. This is stored to respond to your inquiry.

If you create an account, we store your username, email, and profile information through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth).

We use analytics tools (Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics) to understand site traffic patterns. This data is anonymized and does not identify you personally.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to operate and improve Juan's World. Specifically:

  • Responding to messages sent through the contact form
  • Authenticating users who create accounts
  • Understanding how visitors use the site
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security enabled. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with industry-standard encryption.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. Analytics cookies may be used by third-party services (Google Analytics). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. Contact us through the Ask Juan form to request data deletion or export.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the site evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the Ask Juan form.
