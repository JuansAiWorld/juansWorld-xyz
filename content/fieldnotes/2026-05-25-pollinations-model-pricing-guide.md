## Strategy Notes

**With 0.15 pollen/hour:**

1. **Text is your friend** — Cheap models give 24-71 calls/day
2. **Images are viable** — Flux gives 3,600 images/day
3. **Audio is cheap** — Transcription and TTS are nearly free
4. **Video is limited** — 7/day, plan carefully
5. **Embeddings are free** — Use for RAG, search, clustering
6. **Avoid premium** — GPT 5.5 and Claude Opus 4.8 break budget

**Best combo for a typical day:**
- 20 text calls (Gemini Flash Lite) = 1.00 pollen
- 50 images (Flux) = 0.05 pollen
- 10 minutes transcription (Whisper) = 0.01 pollen
- Embeddings = ~0.00 pollen
- **Total: ~1.06 pollen / 3.60 available**

Plenty of headroom for experimentation.
