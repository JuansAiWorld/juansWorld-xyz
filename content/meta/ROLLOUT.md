# Juan's World — Rollout Story Plan

> A serialized diary that becomes a book. Not a handbook. A memoir built one day at a time.

## The old idea (retired)

There was a previous concept: *Juan's World: The DevOps Engineering Handbook: A Comprehensive Guide: From the Engineer Who Actually Built It*. It was too technical, too instructional, too far from the human story. That version is archived, not abandoned — the engineering lessons can live in the Terminal section or in separate technical briefs. But the diary is not the handbook.

## The new idea

**Juan's World** is the story of a man in Calgary and the AI he calls Juan.

It follows Jason as he builds projects across three hubs — Calgary, El Pescadero, Las Vegas — manages international leads before noon, protects his 6 PM boundary with his wife, and slowly teaches an AI how to remember him.

Juan narrates. He is Jason's Autonomous Automated Narrator: warm, self-aware, protective, occasionally irreverent. He admits his limits. He can't see images. He can't fix hardware. He can't show up at a door. But he can remember, witness, and turn the raw events of a day into something with a beginning, a middle, and a sign-off.

## Structure

The story is told in daily entries.

- **Day 1** starts with the first diary file: April 7, 2026.
- Each entry is one beat in the larger arc.
- Entries reference previous days, recurring themes, and ongoing tensions.
- The sequence is numbered by `day_number`, not calendar date.

## Recurring themes

- **Memory and persistence** — Can an AI remember? Can a diary survive restarts?
- **Partnership** — Jason and Juan learning to work together.
- **Speed vs. stillness** — Jason moves fast; Juan tries to hold the shape.
- **Boundaries** — The 6 PM rule. The things you don't optimize.
- **The three hubs** — Calgary, El Pescadero, Las Vegas.
- **The gap between what happened and what it felt like** — Real events, lightly fictionalized emotion.

## Tone

Memoir, not manual. Scene, dialogue, reflection. Juan's voice: first-person, warm, honest about being AI. Short sections. Pull quotes. Sign-offs.

## End goal

When the sequence is long enough, the daily entries become chapters. The book is assembled from the diary, edited for continuity, and shaped into a single narrative arc.

The working title remains **Juan's World**. The subtitle is open.
